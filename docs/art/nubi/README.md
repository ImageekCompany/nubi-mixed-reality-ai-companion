# Nubi: companion character

Nubi is the project's original mascot. It is a small coral seedling-bean with a leaf sprout. The
mesh, UVs, texture, rig, weights, shape keys and animations are all generated by one Blender
Python script. No external assets are used.

![three-quarter](nubi_three_quarter.png)

## Rebuild (one command)

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup \
    --python tools/blender/build_nubi.py            # add `-- --no-render` to skip previews (~6 s)
```

Built and verified with Blender 5.1.0. A full run with previews takes about 40 s. The build is
deterministic, so a rebuild overwrites these files:

| Output | Path |
|---|---|
| Unity FBX | `JollyXR/Assets/Jolly/Art/Character/Nubi.fbx` |
| Per-clip face presets | `JollyXR/Assets/Jolly/Art/Character/Nubi_FacePresets.json` |
| Albedo texture (1024², sRGB) | `JollyXR/Assets/Jolly/Art/Character/Textures/T_Nubi_Albedo.png` |
| Editable scene (git-ignored) | `tools/blender/out/Nubi.blend` |
| Previews and validation report | `docs/art/nubi/*.png`, `docs/art/nubi/build_report.json` |

Source: `tools/blender/build_nubi.py` is the entry point. The `tools/blender/nubi/` package holds:

- `config` for all dimensions and colours
- `geometry`, `rig`, `shapes`, `texture`
- `faces` for the per-clip face presets (drives the clip previews and the JSON above)
- `posing` for character-space FK, two-bone IK and channel conversion
- `clips` for the 13 animations as functions of frame
- `animation`, `validate`, `export`, `render`, `previews`

## Design rationale

- **Silhouette:** a gumdrop body with a soft crown, plus a two-leaf seedling sprout. The sprout is
  what makes the outline Nubi rather than "an egg", so it is sized to read at 1.5–2 m: leaves
  17 × 9 cm, stem 11 cm (1.5× and 1.3× the first pass). It also gives free follow-through animation.
- **Face:** big glossy dome eyes with fixed highlights, rosy cheeks and a clear dark mouth on smooth
  skin, so visemes read from 2–3 m away.
- **Palette:** warm coral body (#F27861) with a cream belly and mittens. Coral stands out against the
  neutral walls and floors of most rooms in passthrough, and it is far from the white/grey Jolly
  look. The leaf green (#5DBB4A, darkened and saturated in round 2 so it holds against bright
  passthrough) is the complementary accent. Cheeks are a rose pink (#F54A6B at 0.9 opacity); the
  earlier coral-pink at 0.65 sat too close to the skin hue and vanished at room distance.
- **Proportions:** about 1.0 m tall including the sprout (0.86 m body), with short legs and stubby mitten arms.
  The scale suits a companion that walks next to you, and is set in Unity anyway.

### How it differs from Meta's Jolly

Jolly is a white/grey furry hooded yeti. Its recessed oval face-plate is framed by the hood, it has
small dot eyes and no mouth. Nubi has none of these:

- no hood
- no face-plate (the face sits directly on smooth skin)
- no fur
- a different body shape (a tapered bean, not a hooded rounded rectangle)
- a different colour family
- a leaf sprout, a belly patch, cheeks, big highlighted eyes and an animated mouth

The Jolly reference renders were only used to steer away from that design. Nothing was traced or
derived from the Jolly mesh. Meta's name and logo are not used as branding.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Triangles | **25,248** (one skinned mesh, 12,683 verts). Quad-dominant Catmull-Clark cages with subdivision applied, smooth shaded |
| Materials | 2, sharing one texture: `M_Nubi_Skin` (roughness 0.62) and `M_Nubi_Gloss` (eyes only, roughness 0.12). Principled BSDF with only Base Color textured, so it maps directly to URP Lit/SimpleLit |
| Texture | `T_Nubi_Albedo.png`, 1024², painted procedurally per texel (no bake). Colour is in the texture, not vertex colours, because URP Lit ignores vertex colours |
| UV coverage | 56 % of the 1024² sheet |
| UVs | Smart-project islands scaled into [0, 0.9]². The mouth has a fixed 0.09² square mapped from its (u, v) grid, so open visemes don't smear a sliver of texture |
| Bones (22) | `Root, Hips, Spine, Chest, Neck, Head, Sprout_1, Sprout_2, Eye_L/R, UpperArm/LowerArm/Hand_L/R, UpperLeg/LowerLeg/Foot_L/R` |
| Skinning | Analytic per-part weights (each part blends only along its own chain). 0 unweighted verts, ≤ 4 influences, all normalised |
| Shape keys | Visemes `sil, A, I, U, E, O`; expressions `Smile, Frown`; eyes `Blink_L, Blink_R, Happy_Eyes, Wide_Eyes, Sad_Eyes` |
| Clips (30 fps) | Loops: `Idle` (100 f), `Walk` (20 f, in place, 0.49 m/s), `Hop` (16 f, in place, 0.90 m/s), `Talk` (60), `Listen` (90), `Think` (90), `Sad` (90). One-shots: `Wave` (60), `Point` (45, ends holding the point), `Happy` (36, hop), `Nod` (30), `ShakeHead` (36), `Surprised` (40), `Shrug` (40), `Stretch` (60, a yawn; idle wake-up) |

FBX facts, checked by re-import and by dumping the FBX tree:

- One take per clip, named exactly as listed above. "Shake head" is spelled `ShakeHead`.
- UnitScaleFactor 100 (1 unit = 1 m), Y up, and Nubi faces Unity +Z.
- No leaf bones.
- Takes contain bone curves only. Shape-key curves are deliberately removed (`export.py`), so the
  Animator never overwrites the lip-sync or blink weights. Faces come from the preset table instead
  (see below).
- `Nod` and `ShakeHead` key a squash **scale** on `Spine`. Every bone has scale keys in every take
  (1.0 elsewhere), so a squash never leaks into the next clip.
- Root is never animated (root motion off). Hips carry the bob and lean.
- Feet are IK-planted in every clip.

Animation principles in `clips.py`:

- every segment is smoothstep-eased
- anticipation on all one-shots
- overlap via per-bone lags
- the sprout always trails the head
- leans and twists come from Hips (a rigid tilt of the blob) to avoid belly creases
- `Nod` and `ShakeHead` spread the motion down the whole chain (Hips 15–20 %, Chest, Neck, Head) and
  squash `Spine` on the downbeats. The "head" is the whole upper blob, so a head-only turn did not
  read. Peak head-chain pitch is about 38° (it was about 20°), and peak yaw is about 38° (it was about 18°)
- `Walk` is a bouncy toddler waddle at 3 steps/s: 18 cm planted stride, a 2.7 cm crouch with
  ±1.1 cm bob, ±14° pelvis yaw (counter-twisted in Spine/Chest so the face stays forward), ±6° roll
  over the stance foot, ±25° arm swing, and the sprout streaming back and bouncing behind the bob
- `Hop` is the catch-up gait: two-footed bunny hops, 5 planted frames out of 16, 9 cm apex,
  arms flung out at the top
- `Think` tilts the head chain ~14° (Hips → Head) into the raised right hand and turns the right
  cheek back towards it. The mitten lies upright against the side of the lower face; its IK target
  is authored in Head space (`PoseSpec.hand_ik_space`) so the contact holds through the head drift
  and the two taps, and the elbow pole drops forward/down (`hand_ik_pole`) so the forearm rises
  along the face instead of stabbing in from the side. Eye bones look up and away (−7° pitch, +9° yaw)

## Validation (from `build_report.json`, regenerated every build)

- Triangle budget is within 15–30k. There are no unweighted vertices.
- **Interpenetration:** every 2nd frame of every clip, arm/hand vertices are tested against the
  posed body shell, and the left foot against the right. Tolerance is 4 mm; the worst normal case
  is 3.6 mm in `Point`. `Think` has its own 32 mm limit and measures 29 mm: the cheek is ~25 cm in
  front of the shoulder, so the upper arm runs inside the side of the blob and the forearm emerges
  from it like a stub limb (checked from front, 3/4 and side). No mitten or tip pokes through the face.
  All clips pass.
- **Locomotion** (`locomotion` in the report): the planted foot slides back at exactly the design
  speed (Walk 0.491 m/s, Hop 0.900 m/s) and the leg IK hits every foot target (Walk 0.0 mm error,
  Hop 1.95 mm at the apex while airborne). Out-of-reach targets would show up here as foot sliding.
- **Mouth:** the neutral and `sil` lip lines are 1.5× thicker than before (9.8 mm and 8.3 mm at the
  centre, previously 6.5 mm and 5.5 mm), so the resting mouth no longer reads as a scratch at room
  distance. The open visemes are unchanged.
- **Eye expressions:** `Wide_Eyes` scales each dome 1.15× in the face plane and 1.06× in depth.
  `Sad_Eyes` compresses the dome under a lid line that is high at the inner corner and drops to the
  outer corner, and lowers the eye by 8 % of its height. The result is a "puppy" droop.
- **Visemes:** `I` is a wide, flat slot (half-width 6.1 cm, 1 cm drop) and `E` is narrower and
  more open (4.6 cm, 3.9 cm drop), so the pair no longer looks the same in close-ups. `sil` stays
  close to neutral on purpose: silence should look like a resting mouth.
- **Blink:** the closed eye is 0.21 of its open height. It becomes a thin lid line (see
  `nubi_face_shapes.png`).
- The previews were reviewed by eye:
  - turnaround: `nubi_front/three_quarter/side/back.png`
  - face sheet with all shape keys and two eye-bone look poses: `nubi_face_shapes.png`
  - per-clip contact sheets: `nubi_clip_*.png`

## Unity notes for the integrator

- **Import:**
  - Rig: **Generic**, with Root node `Root`. Humanoid has not been tried (UNVERIFIED), and the
    chibi proportions retarget poorly.
  - Enable **Bake Axis Conversion**. Otherwise the `Nubi_Rig` child keeps a −90° X rotation that
    the Root bone compensates (visually correct either way).
  - Set **Loop Time** on Idle, Walk, Hop, Talk, Listen, Think and Sad.
  - **Not done yet:** the current `Nubi.fbx.meta` still has `bakeAxisConversion: 0` and
    `clipAnimations: []`, so the loops would play once and freeze. Set these in an
    `AssetPostprocessor` (`OnPreprocessModel` / `OnPreprocessAnimation`) so they live in code.
- **Face presets (required for emotion clips to read):** load `Nubi_FacePresets.json`. When a clip
  starts, crossfade its preset weights in over ~0.15 s (×100 for `SetBlendShapeWeight`). Hold them
  for one-shots and fade them out when the clip ends. Lip-sync visemes and blinks are added on top
  and clamped to 100. While `Happy_Eyes` is above 0.5, suppress blinks. Table:

  | Clip | Preset | Clip | Preset |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Idle | — | Point | Smile 0.3, Wide_Eyes 0.3 |
  | Walk | Smile 0.3 | Happy | Smile 1, Happy_Eyes 1 |
  | Hop | Smile 0.6 | | |
  | Talk | — (lip-sync) | Nod | Smile 0.45 |
  | Listen | Smile 0.35 | ShakeHead | Frown 0.5 |
  | Think | U 0.6 | Sad | Frown 1, Sad_Eyes 1 |
  | Wave | Smile 0.8 | Surprised | O 1, Wide_Eyes 1 |
  | Shrug | Smile 0.25, Wide_Eyes 0.35 | Stretch | O 0.7, Happy_Eyes 1 |

  The clip contact sheets render with these presets applied, so they show the intended result.
- **Grounding:** in passthrough Nubi floats without a contact shadow. Add a blob-shadow decal under
  `Root`, or a shadow-receiving material on the detected floor plane.
- **Depth:** the mouth patch sits 1.5 mm off the skin (`MOUTH_SURFACE_OFFSET`). Keep a 24-bit depth
  buffer. If the mouth flickers on device, raise the offset to 3 mm in `config.py` and rebuild, or
  add a depth offset on the material.
- **Lip-sync:** map uLipSync phonemes A/I/U/E/O/N to the shape keys `A, I, U, E, O, sil` on
  `Nubi_Mesh` (critic.md C6: no jaw bone). Blendshape weights are 0–100 in Unity.
- **Eyes:** blink with `Blink_L/R`. Look-at rotates `Eye_L/R` (pivots sit deep in the head so the
  eyes slide over the face). Keep the look within about ±16° yaw and ±8° pitch: beyond that, the
  outer eye sinks into the side of the head. The look-at controller must therefore turn Hips/Head
  toward the target before the eyes saturate. Use the eyes for the last ~10° only.
  `Think` keys the eye bones itself, so the look-at should blend out while it plays.
- **Locomotion:** `Walk` matches **0.49 m/s** at 1×, and `Hop` matches **0.90 m/s** at 1×. Drive
  Animator speed = agent speed / clip speed, clamped to about 0.7–1.5×. Blend Walk→Hop above about
  0.65 m/s, and cap the agent at about 1.35 m/s (Hop × 1.5). Past 1.5× the feet visibly flicker.
- **Edge readability (shader task):** a flat coral blob can melt into warm-toned rooms. Add a subtle
  rim/fresnel term to `M_Nubi_Skin` (a warm-white rim, kept weak so it doesn't read as a glow) together
  with the blob contact shadow below. Not baked into the texture, because it is view-dependent.
- `Think` is a hand-to-cheek pose with a head tilt into the hand and an upward look. The short arms
  cannot reach the chin on the deep body. Its preset is `U` 0.6, which gives a "hmm" face.

## Open risks

- The Unity-side import (axis, scale and clip names inside Unity) has not been checked in Unity,
  because this pipeline must not run Unity. It is verified only by FBX dump and Blender re-import:
  rotations round-trip within 0.6 mm. Note that Blender's FBX importer auto-connects Hips, which
  hides the Hips translation on re-import. The translation curves are present in the FBX.
- Penetration checks cover arms against the body and foot against foot. Leaves against the head
  and eyes against the lids are checked visually only. `Wide_Eyes` combined with eye look-at at the
  edge of its range was not rendered.
- The `Spine` squash scale is not seen by the FK/IK model in `posing.py`. That is safe today because
  no IK target hangs off `Spine` in Nod or ShakeHead. Adding hand IK to those clips would need scale
  support first.
- The face presets are approximated in the previews as constant weights for the whole clip. Runtime
  fades are the integrator's job.
