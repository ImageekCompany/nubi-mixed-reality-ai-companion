# Jolly: what to try

Jolly is a small plush AI companion that lives in your real room on a Meta Quest 3. You talk to it, show it things, and send it around the room.

## Before you start

- Use a Quest 3 on Wi-Fi, in a lit room with some free floor.
- The app is called **Nubi** in your library (under Unknown Sources).
- Allow the **microphone** and **headset camera** prompts on first launch. Also allow spatial data if asked.
- If you see "API key missing", the app is running offline. See [No internet or no API key](#no-internet-or-no-api-key).
- For better walking around furniture, run the Quest's Space Setup first, or say "Scan my room".

## The two controls

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Pinch and hold** thumb and index finger, speak, then let go | Jolly listens, then answers. On a controller, hold **A** or **X** |
| **Point at the floor** until a ring appears, then **quick pinch** (or pull the controller trigger) | Jolly walks to the ring |

- Wait for Jolly to finish, or pinch while it's talking to interrupt it.
- Answers usually start within 2 to 4 seconds. Searching the web takes a few seconds longer.

## Things to say

These are examples, not commands. Use your own words.

### Talk

- "Hi, who are you?"
- "Tell me a joke."
- "I had a rough day."

### Show it something

Hold an object 40 to 50 cm from your face and point at it with your other hand.

- "What is this?"
- "How do I use this?"
- "Where are my glasses?" (outlines every match it can see)
- "Which button turns it on?" (after it has identified the object; this one is the least reliable)

### Ask about right now

- "What's the weather today?"
- "Any big news today?"
- "I live in Kraków." (Jolly remembers your city. Until then it guesses from the headset's time zone and tells you which place it checked)

### Move it around

- "Come here." / "Come closer."
- "Follow me." ... then walk around ... "Stop."
- "Go stand behind me." / "Stand on my left." / "Back off a bit."
- "Go to the couch." or "Go to the table." (needs a scanned room)
- "Remember this spot as the door." ... later ... "Go to the door."
- "Look at the window."

### Make it perform

- "Show me how to dance!"
- "Jump!" / "Spin around." / "Take a bow." / "Celebrate!"
- "Wave at me." / "Nod." / "Shrug." / "Stretch." / "Look around."
- "Do a little show: jump, spin and take a bow."
- "Surprise me." / "Do a trick."

A short command ("Dance.", "Jump!", "Spin around.", "Take a bow.", "Wave.", "Nod.", "Celebrate!", "Go behind me.", "Stand on my left.", "Back off.") starts at once, and Jolly does the move without saying anything. Anything longer or asked as a question goes through the usual conversation and takes 2-4 s. Pinch during any move and it stops and Jolly listens.

### Change its personality

- "Be my mentor." (step-by-step teacher)
- "Switch to guru." (calm and reflective)
- "Go back to companion." (the default friend)

### Without words

When Jolly is idle, try looking at it for a while, holding out an open palm, or poking it. It may react with a short line.

## Settings

To open the panel, use any of these:
- Turn your **left palm toward you** and press the wrist button
- Press the left controller's **☰**
- Say **"Open settings"**

It closes with its X, when you start talking, or after 20 seconds.

Inside you'll find caption size, speech speed, volume, seated mode, push-to-talk hand, personality, language, clear memory and API usage.

**Seated mode** is for trying it from a chair: Jolly still comes to you and moves around you, but won't walk off to other places or follow you.

## No internet or no API key

Jolly doesn't freeze. It plays short built-in lines and shows captions until the connection returns. Web search, object recognition and free conversation need the connection.

## Known rough edges

- "Can you jump?" sometimes gets an answer in words. "Jump!" is more reliable.
- A long sentence mixing moves and walking can cut a move short. Saying "Come here and jump" works better than "Jump and come here".
- "Go behind me" needs about 1 m of free floor there; otherwise Jolly says it can't get there.
- Hands and real objects should hide Jolly when they're in front of it; the edges may look rough.
- Web answers come from live search, so they can be wrong or for the wrong town.

## Privacy

Your speech, your questions and any camera frames you ask about are sent to Meta's AI services to produce the answer. Camera frames leave the headset only when you ask Jolly to look at or find something. "Clear memory" in settings erases what it remembers.

For anything odd, send what you said, what Jolly did, and roughly when. Most useful of all: **what made you smile, and what made you stop believing it was in your room?**
